# Long-Lead Equipment

Typical manufacturing lead times for common long-lead items, from industry practice. Use these only as notes on a row. Do not put them in the lead time cell as known values, because real lead times depend on the manufacturer, the market and the order date. Always tell the person to confirm with the vendor.

| Item | Typical range | Submit priority |
|---|---|---|
| Custom air handling units | 12 to 20 weeks | 1 |
| Chillers | 16 to 24 weeks | 1 |
| Generators | 20 weeks or more | 1 |
| Custom switchgear | 16 to 20 weeks | 1 |
| Ductwork and piping systems (for coordination) | n/a | 2 |
| Electrical distribution | varies | 2 |
| Packaged equipment, pumps, motors, light fixtures | varies | 3 |
| Diffusers, grilles, devices, trim, accessories | short | 4, submit with the related system |

## How to use it

- Add a note on the row, for example: `Long-lead item. Typically 12 to 20 weeks, confirm with vendor.`
- Sort or flag priority 1 items first when you summarize for the person. These are the submittals that most often hold up the schedule.
- Fill `lead_weeks` only from a value the spec, the person or a vendor quote supplies.

Source for the ranges: Pelles, "Submittal Register in 30 Minutes" (https://www.pelles.ai/university/articles/submittal-register-30-minutes).
